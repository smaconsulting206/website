The Ceiling

You've probably already had a go.

A free government workshop. An afternoon with a chatbot. A well-meaning attempt by someone on the team who reads about AI in their spare time. All useful for getting started, and all reaching the same ceiling: generic tools, generic advice, and no real way to tell whether the problem is the technology, or something underneath it.

We've watched a great deal of AI spend disappear over the past two years into tools nobody kept using. Most of it wasn't a technology problem.

The Approach

We take a reading across the whole business, not just the technology.

Before anyone mentions AI, we look at four things together: where you're trying to get to, how your people work, how work actually happens day to day, and what your current tools and technology can and can't do. Most AI advice looks at one of these. We look at all four, because that's usually where the real answer is. Governance runs across all of them.

01
Strategy

What growth actually looks like for you, and what's realistic in the next year or two.

02
People

How your team works today, and what they'll need to work well alongside new tools.

03
Process

Where time, quality or money quietly leak out of how things get done.

04
Technology

What your current systems can do, and where AI could genuinely help, or where it can't.

And across all four — governance

Adoption without control is just exposure.

Who is allowed to use what, on which data, checked by whom, and recorded how. We set the control layer alongside the rollout so the organisation can move quickly without losing sight of what its people are putting into a model — and so the position holds up to a client, a regulator or a framework audit.

How We Work Together

A straightforward, four-step way of working.

  1. STEP 01

    Talk it through

    A conversation about where you want the business to be in a year or two. No proposal, no pressure.

  2. STEP 02

    See the full picture

    We look across strategy, people, process and technology, and give you a plain, honest read on what's working and what isn't.

  3. STEP 03

    Agree a plan

    Clear recommendations, some about how you work, some about where AI could help, in the order that suits your budget and pace. You decide what happens next.

  4. STEP 04

    Get support making it happen

    Ongoing guidance as you put the plan into action, so you're never left figuring it out alone, and never paying for a technical team you don't need.

Questions

What people usually ask.

Do you build and deploy the AI systems yourselves?

No. We advise on how AI should be adopted, governed and rolled out, and we stay closely involved while your team or a specialist partner does the building. You will always know exactly who is doing what. The one exception is our fixed-scope AI Bid Acceleration service for bid teams.

Is this just Six Sigma with an AI label on it?

No. Process improvement is part of what we bring, but the diagnostic looks at strategy and people just as closely as process. AI is one possible outcome, not the starting assumption.

How long does the diagnostic take?

Typically two to four weeks, depending on the size of your business and how many people we need to speak to.

We don't have a big budget. Is this still worth a conversation?

Usually, yes. The initial conversation is free, and we'll tell you honestly if what you need is smaller than a full diagnostic.

Do you work outside the UK?

Our roots and most of our client base are UK based, and we do work with businesses further afield where the fit is right. We also hold a place on the Bahrain Tender Board consultancy and advisory framework.
