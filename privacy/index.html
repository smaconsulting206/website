This notice explains how SMA Consulting handles personal data in connection with this website.

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking scripts, and it has no forms. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log your IP address for security and operational purposes. Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when a page loads.

If you email us, we use your name, email address and anything you choose to tell us only to respond to you and, where relevant, to discuss a potential engagement. We do not sell or share this information for marketing.
