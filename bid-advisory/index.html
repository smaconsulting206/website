The problem

Most lost bids are lost before anyone starts writing.

A portal registration that stalls. A missing Carbon Reduction Plan that rules you out at the first gate. A tender chased because it looked close enough, written from scratch by a stretched team, and scored against criteria nobody mapped properly.

Winning public sector work is a system, not a talent. We help you build the system.

Four services, one path

Start where you are. Each step builds on the last.

01 · Get eligible

Bid Readiness & Compliance

Clear the admin barriers that stop good suppliers competing at all.

  • Portal and DPS onboarding: Public Contracts Scotland, PCS-Tender, Proactis, Bloom and regional Dynamic Purchasing Systems
  • Procurement Act 2023 readiness, including Central Digital Platform registration

02 · Win the next bid

Bid Strategy & Review

Senior judgement on which bids to chase and how to win them.

  • Go / No-Go qualification against a realistic view of your chances

  • The Bid War Room: an independent review that scores your draft against the buyer's criteria before you submit

You keep: fewer wasted bids, and stronger ones.

03 · Build your engine

Bid Engine Build

We set up the bid process we use ourselves. Your team runs it.

  • A reviewed, categorised answer library built from your best past bids
  • Quality gates every bid must pass before it moves to the next stage

You keep: a repeatable process that doesn't depend on one person.

AI Bid Acceleration

A fixed-scope, governed setup of an AI bid assistant on tools you already approve.

  • Hands-on training so your team produces first drafts and compliance matrices with it, and checks what it writes
  • Built into your bid workflow, with clear rules on which data goes where

You keep: faster first drafts, with your team still in control.

Questions

What people usually ask.

Will you write our bids for us?

No. We decide with you which bids to go for, design how they should be answered, and review them before submission. Your team writes them, and gets better at it each time.

What if SMA is bidding for the same contract?

We check for conflicts before we start. If we are competing for the same opportunity, we tell you at the outset and don't advise on that bid.

Which portals and frameworks do you cover?

Public Contracts Scotland and PCS-Tender, Proactis, Bloom, the Central Digital Platform and regional Dynamic Purchasing Systems across Scotland and England. If you're facing a different portal, ask us.

How is our data handled in the AI setup?

The assistant runs on tools your organisation already approves. It draws on your answer library when it writes, but your data isn't used to train public models. We agree in writing what it can access, and your team checks everything it produces.

We've never bid for public sector work. Where do we start?

Usually with Bid Readiness & Compliance. It gets you eligible and registered, so you're ready when the right opportunity appears.
